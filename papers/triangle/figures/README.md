# Triangle figures

Extracted from the ASE 2025 PDF (`/assets/publications/ase25/ASE_triangle.pdf`) with pdfimages; images with a soft mask were flattened onto white. No arXiv version exists.

- `manual-vs-triangle-triage.png` (2181x1625): manual triage of a Mac sign-in incident passed from Team A to B to C, versus Triangle auto-triaging with the incident database, monitoring logs and troubleshooting guides. Figure 1, page 2.
- `triage-time-vs-transfer-hops.png` (1752x1151): median triage time units (green) and median human discussion turns (red) per incident by number of transfer hops (1 to 8+), over 3,000+ teams and 12 months. Figure 3, page 3.
- `triangle-framework.png` (2384x1580): the Triangle framework: Analyzer (semantic distillation), Decider (K + N candidates), Team Managers (collaborative triage and negotiation loop) with external environments, ending at the responsible team. Figure 4, page 4.

Table II (hop accuracy, page 7) and Table I (per-service accuracy and TTE reduction, page 6) are tables; the numbers are in `../brief.md` for animation rather than as images.
