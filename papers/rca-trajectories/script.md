# Beyond Fault Localization: one-minute film plan

Built with the presmium-animate story process (`references/story.md`, `storyboards.md` "Papers", `lengths.md` 60 s). Fact source: `highlights.md` (arXiv 2608.21310v2). Figures in `figures/`. The film has no voice: music only, and the pictures plus one short caption at a time carry it. The narration below is kept for a later voiced cut.

## Brief

- Kind: paper video, 60 s, video first (the rest frames double as the paper page's stills); silent except for music.
- Audience: SRE and ML researchers and practitioners who know LLM agents call tools and who read RCA leaderboards (Acc@k).
- Before: "An agent that names the right service diagnosed the incident; one that names the wrong one simply failed."
- After: "An agent's trajectory shows where its diagnosis broke, the breaks fall into three families, and guarding against them raises accuracy everywhere."
- Misconception to confront: the final answer is all that needs grading. Parts to name first: the fault's propagation path (root cause → affected service → symptom), a round (one step of tool calls), the trajectory (the sequence of rounds).
- Assumptions: 16:9; music bed, no voice; captions in English; the end card holds under the last bars.

## Thesis

After watching, the viewer understands **that an RCA agent's trajectory, not its final answer, shows where a diagnosis breaks and what to guard** (19 words), because they saw an agent reach the root cause at step 37 and walk away, correct and failed runs plotted against the fault's path, one incident's six coded failures fall into three families, and a guard against them lift accuracy in all 14 tested setups.

**And–But–Therefore.** RCA agents are scored by the service they name, and the strongest name it in 8 to 9 of 10 incidents; **but** the trajectory behind each answer shows runs that reach the cause and leave, wander off the fault's path, or ignore what their own queries return; therefore the paper grades trajectories against annotated fault paths, sorts the failures into three families, and builds a guard against them that raises Acc@1 in all 14 configurations it tests.

## Pattern

**Claim on trial** (story.md's row for an empirical, analysis or negative-result paper: the takeaway → the accepted belief → the observation that cracks it → a model of when → an intervention that switches it → scope). The paper is a study, not a method or a benchmark: its contribution is what trajectories reveal (the observation), the failure taxonomy (the model of when) and DiagGuard (the intervention that switches it). Gap → instrument → finding would put the annotated paths at the centre, but they are the paper's measuring stick, not its claim; mechanism walk would centre DiagGuard, which the paper itself calls a prototype that tests the taxonomy. Under a minute, the accepted belief and the crack share the opening shot: a scored answer and the trajectory behind it.

## Three storylines

- **A. Footprints on the fault's path (recommended).** Every shot plots where the agent was against where the fault went. The film opens on the scored answer, then the agent's footprints along the annotated path (Figure 1): at steps 37–40 it stands on the root cause and leaves without reading its metrics. The path turns into a depth axis and two runs are drawn round by round on it, one staying on the path, one straying off. A real incident (Figure 6b) shows the failures in place, six coded failures drop into three bins, and DiagGuard wraps the agent; the same incident now ends on the right node and 14 gain bars grow. Form: a chain where each shot's last object is the next one's subject (footprints → dots → coded tags → guard). Rules out: RQ1's framework league table and the intent taxonomy on screen (both stay on the page).
- **B. Two runs, one incident.** A split screen on the TiKV incident: left the baseline agent, right the DiagGuard agent, both starting from the same slow product page. Their trajectories advance in lockstep, diverging at the tier boundary; failure codes stamp the left side as they happen, the right side descends to tidb-tikv-0; the halves merge into the gain chart. Form: a split that becomes one. Rules out: the 5,000-trajectory characterization (the study becomes a before/after demo).
- **C. The flight recorder (bold).** Open on a tape of one failed run, 47 steps long, scrubbed backward until the playhead stops at step 37 where the agent stood on the cause. Pull back: the tape is one of 5,000 in a stacked archive; the failed tapes slide into three drawers labelled by how evidence was lost; a guard clips onto the recorder and replayed tapes end on green. Form: an endless zoom out. Rules out: concrete telemetry of any one incident beyond the first tape.

Recommendation: **A**. It keeps the claim a visible property (where the agent stood versus where the fault went) on every frame, each number is counted off the picture that shows it (step ranges, rounds drawn, coded tags, bar lengths), and it uses the paper's own two worked cases (Figures 1 and 6b) rather than invented ones. The turn (the coded failures falling into three families) lands at about 0:38, 63% in, after which the guard answers each family.

## Picture

- Central picture: a service map with the agent's position marked on it, round by round; the fault's annotated path drawn in ink, the agent's footprints in its colour.
- Money frame: step 4's rest frame, the six coded tags of the TiKV incident sitting in three bins under the map that produced them.
- Colours: teal `#0f766e` = on the fault's path, correct answer, evidence used; vermilion `#b93a0b` = off the path, wrong answer, a coded failure; blue `#2546b8` (the Diagnose thread) = the guard (Grounder, Verifier) and its gains; grey = context and telemetry not yet touched.
- Spatial convention: the root cause sits at the bottom or left, the observed symptom at the top or right; time (steps, rounds) runs left to right.
- LOOK: paper ground `#f6f6f1`, ink outlines 3 px, square boxes with hard offset shadows, Silkscreen for labels and IBM Plex Sans for captions, one caption at a time at the foot. BANNED: dark void with glow, KPI tiles, paragraphs on screen, SQL text.

## Narration

143 words (counted with `wc -w` over the eight narration cells), for a later voiced cut; about 2.5 words a second.

> An AI agent investigates a failing microservice system and answers: the affected service. Wrong, and the score records nothing else. By step 37 it stood on the real root cause, left its metrics unread, and walked back. Plot each round by its distance from the fault's root. Correct runs keep re-querying the path and use what returns. Failed runs stray off the path, ignore what they found, and run longer. Here, the agent trusts the noisiest service, skips its healthy baseline, and never probes storage. The same agent now finds the storage node. Across two benchmarks and six frameworks, accuracy rises in all fourteen setups. Read the trajectory: it shows where the diagnosis broke.
